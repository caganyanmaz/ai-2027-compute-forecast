# AI Futures Project: newer wafer-capacity modeling

Checked 12 November 2026.

A relevant primary source omitted from the earlier research is Romeo Dean's [Compute in Plan A](https://ai-2040.com/supplements/compute-supplement), especially §1.1 and §2.2. It models a shared EUV constraint for logic and HBM and locates near-saturation around 2029–2030. This is a newer treatment, not an explicit retraction of the original 1.25× assumption. Its later scenario-dependent projections require separate interpretation.

The [AI 2040 credits and changelog](https://ai-2040.com/about) establish AI Futures Project authorship, Dean's responsibility for compute modeling, and compute-supplement edits in July 2026. The precise revision date of each current parameter was not established.

The [original forecast](https://ai-2027.com/research/compute-forecast) still states a slowdown from 1.65× to around 1.25× beyond 2027. The [AI Futures Model](https://www.aifuturesmodel.com/p?acam=time-horizon&base=daniel-08-16-26&v=2) also retains fab/investment constraints, but describes leading-company compute, a different quantity.

Research correction: earlier reports should not be read as establishing that no newer author-produced physical-supply model exists. Compare the newly located supplement before selecting post-2027 parameters. It has not been reproduced or independently validated here. Do not mix its performance normalization with our dense-FP8 series without reconciliation.
